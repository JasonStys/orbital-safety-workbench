Scientific assumptions & limitations

    GETTING STARTED

    Small controls.
    Explicit assumptions.

    1 / Choose a scenario

    The ISS replay uses a public May 2024 mean-element record and SGP4. The two synthetic cases are fictional, sampled histories—not orbital dynamics models.

    2 / Inspect the evidence

    Scrub elapsed time, select an object, or play at 60 replay seconds per real second. The chart, coordinates and speed follow the same cursor. Closest approaches search every linear segment, not just sampled timestamps.

    3 / Export a reproducible record

    JSON and HTML include full histories, units, frame, source hash, model and limitations. CSV provides unit-labeled samples linked to the same run ID. Results are not uploaded. Only scenario/window preferences stay in your browser.

    Not a safety decision tool. No live catalog, covariance, collision probability, operational alerts, targeting or maneuver planning. Consult qualified orbital analysts for real operations.
